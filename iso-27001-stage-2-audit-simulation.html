Stage 2 Certification Audit Simulation

A complete audit lifecycle for Northstar Secure Cloud, a fictional encrypted cloud-storage provider—from risk-based planning and team leadership through evidence evaluation, finding classification, closing, corrective action, and follow-up verification.

Engagement overview

Objectives, scope and criteria

The simulation tested whether an ISMS was implemented, maintained, and capable of supporting certification readiness through objective evidence.

Audit objectives

What the audit evaluated

  • Conformity with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clauses 4–10.
  • Implementation and effectiveness of selected Annex A controls.
  • Whether evidence supported a defensible audit conclusion.
  • Whether identified issues were correctly classified and followed through.
Scope and criteria

Boundaries of the assessment

  • Development, maintenance, and operation of an encrypted cloud-storage service.
  • ISMS processes, roles, records, risks, and selected technical and operational controls.
  • ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and the organization's own documented requirements.
  • Sampling limitations were communicated; third-party infrastructure and customer endpoints were outside the operational boundary.
Audit execution

Risk-based lifecycle

Each stage was documented so that conclusions could be traced back to criteria and objective evidence.

Plan

Defined objectives, scope, criteria, sampling, timing, roles, and priority controls.

Lead

Allocated work, briefed the team, addressed objectivity risks, and chaired meetings.

Evaluate

Reviewed procedures, records, interviews, risk information, and control evidence.

Report

Recorded conformities, nonconformities, opportunities for improvement, and limitations.

Follow up

Defined correction, root cause, corrective action, evidence, and effectiveness checks.

Evidence and results

Corrective action

From finding to verified closure

The follow-up plan separates immediate containment from the action needed to remove the underlying cause and prevent recurrence.

Correction

Keep the three devices in secure, access-controlled storage and update the equipment register until approved disposal is completed.

Root cause

Procedure ownership, approval deadlines, disposal methods, supplier requirements, and retained-record expectations were not clearly assigned.

Corrective action

Approve and implement the procedure, assign responsibility, train personnel, process the devices, and retain erasure or destruction evidence.

Verification

Review the approved procedure and register, sample disposal records and certificates, confirm implementation, and test effectiveness over time.

Audit conclusion

Certification recommendation subject to satisfactory closure

The sampled ISMS was generally implemented and aligned with the selected requirements, with one minor nonconformity requiring correction and corrective action. A certification recommendation would remain subject to satisfactory treatment of the finding and the certification body's process. The certification body—not the audit team—retains authority for the final certification decision.
