Working Principles

How I turn evidence into action.

Four principles that shape every assessment, finding, and recommendation in this portfolio — and that carry over directly into how I'd operate as an analyst on a real team.

Principle 01

Evidence Before Opinion

Every finding traces back to a specific observation — a log setting, an account list, a policy gap — not a general impression. If it isn't documented, it isn't a finding.

Principle 02

Frameworks as a Shared Language

NIST CSF, ISO 27001, and CIS Controls give findings a common vocabulary auditors, engineers, and executives can all work from — so citations are explicit, never implied.

Principle 03

Technical Fluency Behind Governance

SOC-level exposure to logs, incidents, and indicators means control gaps get assessed with a working understanding of what the underlying system is actually doing.

Principle 04

Human Judgment, AI-Assisted

AI tooling can accelerate drafting and pattern recognition, but every recommendation in this portfolio is reviewed and owned by a person before it's called finished.

01 · Identify

Scope & Environment

02 · Assess

Evidence Review

03 · Document

Write structured findings with observations, risks, framework citations, and maturity notes aligned to controls.

04 · Remediate

Prioritized Actions

Recommend prioritized remediation actions with named owners, urgency tiers, and effort estimates.

05 · Monitor

Ongoing Improvement

Support continuous improvement through review schedules and ongoing audit readiness tracking.

Program Maturity

Before and after the GRC assessment series.

Before Assessment

Initial State — Control Gaps

  • Three former employee accounts still active in M365
  • MFA not enforced for 14 of 22 users
  • No alert policies configured — zero detection capability
  • Backup restoration never tested
  • Two EOL Windows 10 devices, three with patches 45–91 days late

After Recommendations

Improvement Roadmap — Defined Outputs

  • Immediate account revocation + offboarding checklist documented
  • M365 Conditional Access MFA enforcement recommended
  • 5 alert policies defined; log retention path recommended
  • Quarterly backup test procedure and OneDrive KFM policy proposed
  • EOL upgrade path and patch cadence SLA established
  • Policy template, training platform, vendor clause, and IR contact sheet defined
